# Phase 13 — After-Launch ASO & 30-Day Growth Plan

**Application:** StandUp: Habit Alarm & Tracker (`com.growthstreaks.standup`)  
**Target Marketplace:** PhonePe Appstore (India)

---

## 1. 30-Day Launch Execution Roadmap

### Days 1 to 7: Launch Stabilization & Performance Monitoring
- **Crash Rate Tracking:** Monitor PhonePe Developer Portal crash analytics and Google AdMob dashboard for SDK telemetry.
- **Reviewer Feedback:** Address any initial user ratings within 24 hours.
- **OEM Battery Killers Watch:** Check user feedback regarding aggressive battery killer OEMs (Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS, OnePlus OxygenOS, Vivo FuntouchOS, Samsung OneUI). If users report alarms silenced by battery savers, update in-app onboarding hints for "Auto-Start" and "Disable Battery Saver".
- **Initial Verification:** Confirm real-world AdMob impressions and eCPMs across Indian Tier-1/Tier-2 traffic.

### Days 8 to 14: Search Keyword Discovery & Ranking Audit
- **PhonePe Appstore Search Rankings:** Search for primary keywords on the PhonePe Appstore:
  - `Alarm Clock`, `Smart Alarm`, `Mission Alarm`
  - `Step Counter`, `Pedometer`, `Walking Tracker`
  - `Habit Tracker`, `Daily Routine Planner`
- **Keyword Trajectory:** Note search position and ranking changes. If `Pedometer` or `Habit Tracker` shows higher velocity, adjust the subtitle or keyword density in the PhonePe Developer Portal.

### Days 15 to 30: Conversion Rate Optimization & Review Acquisition
- **Screenshot A/B Evaluation:** Test swapping Slot 1 between `01_dashboard_accountability.jpg` (Discipline Focus) and `02_routines_and_camera_alarm.jpg` (Camera Alarm Feature Focus) to measure click-through conversion changes.
- **In-App Rating Prompt Strategy:** Trigger an optional, non-intrusive rating prompt after the user achieves a **3-day streak** in StandUp (the peak moment of satisfaction and perceived value).
- **AdMob Revenue Optimization:** Analyze AdMob waterfall and eCPM floors for Indian inventory; adjust frequency capping on interstitial ads to protect user retention.

---

## 2. Long-Term Growth Levers for StandUp in India
1. **Regional Festivals & New Year Habits:** Capitalize on New Year resolutions (January), exam preparation routines (February-March), and morning fitness drives with targeted localized promotional copy.
2. **Word-of-Mouth Habit Sharing:** Add a native screenshot sharing feature allowing users to share their monthly streak calendar on WhatsApp and Instagram Stories with their daily step count.
