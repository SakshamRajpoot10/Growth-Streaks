Four Pillars of Consistent Growth

Traditional alarm clocks are too easy to snooze, and standalone habit apps are too easy to forget. StandUp unifies waking up, moving, and tracking into one seamless OS.

⏰

Native Alarm Engine

Powered by native Android background services. Rings reliably over the lock screen, even when the app is swiped away or the phone is in deep sleep. Uses isolated STREAM_ALARM audio channels so it is never muted by media volume sliders.

📸

30s Camera Verification

Physically forces you out of bed. To disarm your morning alarm, StandUp requires a 30-second upright standing posture check using the front camera. Processed 100% in volatile RAM — zero photos are captured, saved, or uploaded.

🏃

24/7 Hardware Pedometer

Tracks your daily steps, active minutes, estimated distance, and burned calories automatically using device hardware sensors and Google Play Services Local Recording API — without battery-draining GPS or persistent notification clutter.

📅

Habit Streak Calendar

Monitor your daily routines and monthly momentum. Days where all morning tasks and alarms are completed light up with vibrant completion markers to keep your consistency streaks unbroken week after week.
