Four Pillars of Daily Consistency

Traditional alarms are too easy to silence, and standalone habit trackers are easy to forget. StandUp unites waking up, daily movement, and streak tracking into one seamless application.

⏰

Smart Alarm Engine

Built directly into Android's native system alarm services. Rings reliably over your locked screen, even when other apps are open or the phone is in deep sleep. Uses dedicated alarm audio channels so it is never accidentally muted by media sliders.

📸

30-Second Wake-Up Mission

Put an end to accidental snoozing. To dismiss your morning alarm, StandUp requires a 30-second upright standing posture check using the front camera. Processed 100% in temporary device memory — zero photos are captured or saved.

🏃

24/7 Background Pedometer

Measures your daily walking steps, active minutes, estimated distance, and burned calories automatically using hardware motion sensors — completely avoiding battery-draining GPS tracking or annoying permanent notifications.

📅

Habit Streak Calendar

Visualize your monthly consistency at a glance. Days where all morning tasks and routines are completed light up with vibrant completion markers, motivating you to keep your momentum going week after week.
