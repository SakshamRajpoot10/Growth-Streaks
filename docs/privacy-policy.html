Privacy Policy for StandUp

Application: StandUp: Habit Alarm & Tracker • Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Publisher: Saksham Rajpoot

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how StandUp: Habit Alarm & Tracker ("StandUp", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by Saksham Rajpoot, collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

StandUp is designed as a daily discipline, habit tracking, and waking accountability application. We operate on a strict privacy-first, on-device architecture: we do not maintain user accounts, we do not require your phone number or email address to use the app, and we do not store your private habits or physical routines on remote servers.

2. Core Privacy Commitment: 100% On-Device Processing

Zero Account Requirement & Local Storage: StandUp does not require registration, login credentials, or cloud synchronization. All your scheduled alarms, custom routine names, daily streak progress, and step counts are stored strictly on your physical phone within local device storage.

3. Information We Process

A. Physical Activity & Daily Steps

StandUp measures your daily walking steps, estimated distance, active minutes, and calories burned using your smartphone's built-in hardware step sensor and standard Android system activity recognition.

  • On-Device Storage: All step records and historical trends are saved exclusively in a local database on your device.
  • No Cloud Upload: Your physical movement records are never sent to external servers or shared with third parties.
  • Zero Location Tracking: StandUp does not request, track, or record your physical GPS location.

B. Camera Wake-Up Verification (Ephemeral Processing)

When you enable a mission-based morning alarm, StandUp offers an interactive 30-second upright standing posture check via your front camera to ensure you have physically gotten out of bed before turning off the alarm.

  • In-Memory (RAM) Processing Only: Camera video frames are analyzed in real-time within your device's temporary memory solely to verify posture.
  • Zero Image or Video Storage: No photographs, facial recordings, or biometric templates are captured, saved to internal storage, or uploaded to any server.
  • Immediate Hardware Release: The camera session automatically shuts down and releases device access the exact second the verification finishes or the alarm is turned off.

C. Morning Routines & Streak Calendar Data

Your habit preferences, scheduled alarm times, custom ringtone choices, and monthly streak completions are stored locally on your device. If you clear the application data in Android settings or uninstall StandUp, this information is completely deleted.

4. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

StandUp requests only the standard Android operating system permissions strictly necessary to perform its core alarm, habit, and pedometer functions:

Permission Purpose in StandUp Data Handling & Destination
Alarm Scheduling Allows scheduled alarms to ring at the exact minute set by the user. Processed locally by the Android system Alarm Manager.
Full-Screen Wakeup Displays the wake-up alarm screen directly over your locked screen so you can wake up immediately. Displayed locally on your phone screen.
Camera Access Used solely for the 30-second upright posture wake-up check to disarm the alarm. Analyzed temporarily in device memory. No photos are saved or uploaded.
Physical Activity Permits reading daily walking step counts from your device's built-in motion sensor. Saved in the local database on your device only.
Background Sensor Ensures your daily walking steps continue recording without interruptions throughout the day. Operates locally on your device.
Audio & Sound Plays your chosen wake-up ringtone through the phone's dedicated alarm channel until dismissed. Plays through your device speakers.
Device Startup Automatically restores and reschedules your active alarms if your phone restarts or updates. Restored from your local database on restart.
Notifications Sends daily routine reminders and active alarm notifications. Appears locally in your phone's notification drawer.
Internet Access Required exclusively to load non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob. Communicates directly with Google AdMob servers.

5. Advertising & Google AdMob

StandUp is a free application supported by non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, Publisher ID: pub-9675414045566238).

  • Data Collected by AdMob: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically receive coarse device technical signals, IP address, and your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) to serve ads, prevent fraud, and manage ad frequency in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Alarm Safety Guarantee: Advertisements are strictly suppressed and blocked whenever an alarm is ringing or camera verification is active, ensuring your morning wake-up is never disrupted.
  • User Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android phone settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, daily habits, waking times, camera feed, or physical step records with any external company. Except for the standard advertising data processed directly by Google AdMob, no information leaves your mobile device.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Because StandUp stores your data locally on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over data deletion:

  • Clearing Storage: You can instantly wipe all saved alarms, streak history, and step logs at any time through your phone: Settings → Apps → StandUp → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the StandUp application permanently deletes all local databases, custom routines, and configuration files from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

StandUp is rated for Everyone (3+) as a general discipline, alarm, and walking step tracker. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since our application does not feature account registration, no child's personal identifying information is ever collected.

9. Compliance with Indian Regulations

StandUp complies fully with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India by practicing complete data minimization, processing camera feeds ephemerally without storage, and keeping personal wellness records under the user's sole physical custody on their device.
