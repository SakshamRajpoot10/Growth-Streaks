Privacy Policy for StandUp

Effective Date: November 2026
Applicable Law: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) & Global Standards
Application: StandUp — Discipline & Habit Alarm OS (com.growthstreaks.standup)

1. Privacy-First & On-Device Processing

StandUp is designed as a privacy-first, on-device discipline and habit operating system. We believe your daily habits, wake-up verification, and movement data belong to you alone.

Core Commitment: All primary features function 100% on-device. We do not maintain remote user databases, do not require user account logins, and do not transmit your habit logs, alarms, or camera frames to any remote server.

2. Data We Process

A. Motion & Step Tracking

Daily step counts, active minutes, and estimated distance/calories are collected via your phone's hardware step sensor (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) and Google Play Services Local Recording API. Data is saved strictly in a local SQLite database on your physical device and is never uploaded to any cloud server.

B. Camera Wake-Up Verification

During the 30-second upright standing wake-up check, camera frames are analyzed ephemerally in device RAM to verify standing posture. Zero photos, videos, or facial templates are stored on disk or transmitted over the internet. The session terminates immediately upon alarm disarming.

C. Google AdMob (Third-Party Advertising)

StandUp displays non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-9675414045566238). Google may collect your Google Advertising ID (AAID) and coarse device signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Safety Lock: Ads are programmatically blocked while alarms are ringing or camera verification is active.

Clearing application storage or uninstalling the StandUp app permanently deletes all local databases, streak records, and settings from your device.

For inquiries or grievance redressal under India's DPDP Act, 2023, please contact our support team at our official developer portal.
